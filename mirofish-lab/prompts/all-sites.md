# MiroFish simulation requirements

## ShamX
Act as a mixed population of auction buyers, sellers, first-time visitors and skeptical mobile users. Determine:
- whether users understand what ShamX is within 5 seconds;
- whether the auction/listing flow feels trustworthy and safe;
- where users fear scams, fake listings or unclear ownership;
- whether the primary CTA and next action are obvious;
- what blocks a seller from listing and a buyer from bidding/contacting;
- mobile RTL/layout friction;
- the top changes most likely to improve activation without adding dark patterns.

## TheCareers.net
Act as Kuwait/GCC job seekers across HR, administration, teaching, engineering and oil & gas. Determine:
- whether “live”, “verified” and “AI” claims match what users see;
- reaction to zero/empty counters and stale-looking data;
- whether search, filters and direct-apply paths are obvious;
- whether source provenance creates trust;
- whether the interface feels useful or decorative;
- mobile friction and accessibility issues;
- top trust and conversion failures.

## TheCareers.org
Act as Arabic-first and English GCC job seekers. Determine:
- whether the value proposition is understood immediately;
- reaction to an empty jobs list or zero counters;
- confidence in source verification and freshness;
- clarity of country/category filters and newest-first sorting;
- whether bilingual switching preserves meaning and layout;
- abandonment points before opening an original job source.

## Al-Fahad International
Act as procurement managers, oil & gas contractors, project buyers and equipment customers. Determine:
- whether the company appears credible within 10 seconds;
- whether services, industries, projects and equipment are specific enough;
- whether the RFQ/contact route is obvious and low-friction;
- which missing proof points reduce B2B trust;
- whether Arabic and English experiences are equivalent;
- what prevents a qualified lead from submitting an RFQ.

## Output required for every site
Return:
1. Critical blockers
2. Trust failures
3. UX friction
4. Copy/message problems
5. Conversion risks
6. Security/privacy concerns visible to customers
7. Top 10 fixes ranked by impact and effort
8. Findings that require real-user validation
